Clinical Importance

Why Early Diabetes Risk Screening Is Vital

According to the CDC and International Diabetes Federation, over 1 in 3 adults has pre-diabetes, and more than 80% of them do not know it.

Type 2 diabetes develops slowly over years. In early stages, elevated glucose produces no overt symptoms while silent damage to blood vessels, kidneys, and eyes begins.

  • Reversibility: Pre-diabetes is clinically reversible through diet, exercise, and lifestyle changes.
  • Non-Invasive: Identifies elevated risk without requiring an immediate blood draw.
  • Triage Tool: Helps prioritize who urgently needs formal HbA1c laboratory diagnostic tests.
