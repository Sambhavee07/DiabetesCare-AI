Clinical Impact of Threshold Optimization

In disease screening, a False Negative (missed diabetic patient) is far more hazardous than a False Positive (a healthy person recommended for a simple blood test).

By tuning the decision threshold to 0.43 using our inner validation split, we reduced missed diabetic patients from 1,465 down to 1,045 (a 28.7% reduction in missed diagnoses).
