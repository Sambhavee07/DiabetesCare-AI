Recommended Clinical Diagnostic Tests

Discuss these gold-standard laboratory tests with your primary healthcare physician:

HbA1c Test (Glycated Hemoglobin)

Measures your average blood sugar levels over the past 2 to 3 months. Normal: < 5.7% | Pre-diabetes: 5.7%–6.4% | Diabetes: ≥ 6.5%.

Fasting Plasma Glucose (FPG)

Checks blood glucose after an overnight fast (minimum 8 hours). Normal: < 100 mg/dL | Pre-diabetes: 100–125 mg/dL | Diabetes: ≥ 126 mg/dL.

Complete Lipid Panel

Evaluates HDL, LDL, and triglycerides to monitor cardiovascular and metabolic syndrome comorbidities.

Evidence-Based Preventive Guidance

Simple daily modifications that significantly improve insulin sensitivity:

Nutrition & Diet

  • Prioritize whole grains, leafy greens, and lean proteins over ultra-processed carbohydrates.
  • Reduce refined sugars, sweetened beverages, and simple starches that cause insulin spikes.
  • Include healthy fats such as olive oil, nuts, and avocados in moderation.

Physical Activity

  • Target at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity (e.g., brisk walking) per week.
  • Include resistance or strength training 2 days per week to improve skeletal muscle glucose uptake.
  • Break up prolonged periods of sitting with 5-minute movement intervals.

Recovery & Wellness

  • Aim for 7 to 8 hours of restorative sleep; chronic sleep deprivation increases cortisol and insulin resistance.
  • Practice active stress reduction techniques to curb stress-induced glycemic spikes.
  • Monitor blood pressure and cholesterol annually with a licensed physician.
